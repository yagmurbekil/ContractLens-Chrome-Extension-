Privacy Notice

Fictional test document for ContractLens. Updated 2 February 2026.

The Riverside Community Library is a volunteer-run reading room. This notice describes the small amount of information we keep about members of the library and how members can control it. We wrote it in plain language so that every member can understand it.

What we keep

When you join, we record your name and a contact email address so that we can remind you when a borrowed book is due. We record which books you currently have on loan. When a book is returned, the loan record is erased the same day.

Why we keep it

We use your contact email only for due-date reminders and for answering questions you send us. We never use it for newsletters unless you ask to receive one, and you can stop them with a single reply.

Who can see it

Only the two volunteer librarians on duty can see member records. Records are kept on a single computer in the reading room that is locked when the library is closed. Nobody outside the library receives member records.

How long we keep it

If you leave the library, we erase your member record within thirty days. You can ask for your record to be erased at any time, and we will confirm by email once it is done.

Your rights

You may ask to see the information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to erase it. Requests are free of charge and are handled by the volunteer coordinator within two weeks.

Fees

Membership is free. There are no late fees. If a book is lost, we kindly ask you to replace it with a similar book, but this is voluntary.

Questions

If anything in this notice is unclear, please talk to a librarian on duty or write to hello@riverside-library.example. We are happy to explain how we look after member information.
